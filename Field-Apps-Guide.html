Open TRAC Belt

Belt conveyor scale calibration. Records test rounds, auto-calculates % error, and generates professional PDF reports for customers and plant managers.

Result Status Reference
PASS

% Error < 0.5%
Scale is within spec.

MARGINAL

% Error 0.5–1.0%
Adjusted but watch it.

FAIL

% Error ≥ 1.0% — Scale is out of spec. Adjustment required. Document and notify plant manager.

7-Step Calibration Walkthrough
1
Visit Info

Select the scale from your library (or add a new one). Enter tech name, date, and any pre-visit notes.

Scale library saves all your sites — you won't re-enter customer info each visit.
2
Belt Speed

Enter the belt speed in FPM and idler spacing in inches. The app calculates TWPF (test weight per foot) automatically.

3
Zero As Found

Record the zero reading before any adjustment. Use the ± Neg button if the reading is negative — do not type a minus sign.

First round: set the Cal Span As Found here. The field is editable before any rounds are recorded.
4
Test Rounds

Enter start totalizer, run the belt, record end totalizer. Tap Start timer to run the 5-minute countdown.

Keep this screen open while the timer runs — leaving the page pauses the timer. The app will alert you if you navigate away.
Record As Found first, then make adjustments and add rounds to confirm As Left.
5
Zero As Left

Record the zero reading after adjustment. Use ± Neg if negative.

6
Maintenance & Photos

Complete the maintenance checklist. Tap Add photo to capture equipment photos — these embed directly in the Customer Report PDF.

Photos work from your camera or existing gallery. No internet needed — stored on-device.
7
Review & Save

Review the full calibration summary. All rounds, % errors, and PASS/FAIL status display here. Save the visit, then generate your PDFs.

PDF Reports
Customer Report

Full calibration certificate. Includes QR code, PASS/FAIL banner, test rounds table, and any photos taken. Give this to the customer.

Site Summary

Plant manager one-pager. All scales tested in one visit, scorecard, pass/fail totals, and signature lines.

Generating PDFs From the visit summary screen, tap Customer report or Site summary. Your phone's share sheet will open — save as PDF or send directly via email or text.
Calibration Reminders

The home screen shows reminder cards for scales with upcoming or overdue calibration dates. Tap Draft Reminder Email on any site to generate a pre-written email covering all due scales at that location.

Open TRAC Cell

Load cell OHM testing. Records 13 readings per cell, flags cross-bridge deviations, calculates ROC, and generates a professional Customer Report PDF.

Cell Status Reference
PASS

All readings within spec. 10+ readings complete.

REVIEW

Cross-bridge deviation >1% from average. Flag for closer look.

FAIL

Negative resistance or mV reading detected — physically invalid.

PARTIAL

Fewer than 10 readings entered for this cell.

Job Setup
Customer & Scale Info

Enter customer name, scale ID, tech name, date, excitation voltage, mV/V rating, test weight, and cell capacity.

Wire Color Profiles

Set wire colors (+EX, −EX, +SIG, −SIG, Shield) for the load cell model. The app saves this profile — it auto-loads next time you test the same model.

Per-Cell Model Override

On mixed-manufacturer scales (e.g. 7 Mettler + 1 Rice Lake), tap Change ▾ on any cell to set a different model and wire colors for that cell only.

13-Reading Entry Flow
How it works Each cell has 13 sequential readings. Enter a value and tap Enter (or Next) to auto-advance. The app validates each reading in real time and shows a PASS/FAIL/SUSPECT badge instantly.
1
Dead Load (mV DC)

Read this first — deck loaded, no test weight, while the wires are still connected. Must be positive; negative auto-fails.

First on purpose: capture it before anything gets disconnected for the resistance checks.
2
Excitation Resistance (Ω)

Resistance across the excitation wires. Must be positive — negative auto-fails.

3
Signal Resistance (Ω)

Resistance across the signal wires. Must be positive.

4–7
Cross-Bridge Readings (pp / pn / nn / np)

Four bridge readings. If any deviate >1% from their average, all four are flagged purple — cell status becomes REVIEW.

Purple flag = cross-bridge imbalance. Not a hard fail, but document it.
8–9
−Comp / +Comp (Ω)

Compensation resistor readings. Must be positive.

10–11
Shorts — Ω and nS

Insulation check. Shorts Ω: any numeric value = FAIL (OL = PASS). Shorts nS: <0.5 = PASS, 0.5–1.0 = SUSPECT, >1.0 = BAD.

Can't type "OL" on a number pad — use the OL button.
12
Zero Load (mV DC)

Must be between 0 and +0.5 mV. Negative = FAIL.

13
Known Load (mV DC)

ROC is auto-calculated: Known Load − Dead Load. Shown in the on-screen summary and Full Report alongside the theoretical ROC from job setup.

Useful Shortcuts
Copy Cell 1

On any cell other than Cell 1, tap Copy Cell 1 to paste all readings from the first cell. Useful on uniform junction boxes — confirm overwrite when prompted.

Edit from Summary

After completing a cell, tap the edit icon on the summary row to go back and edit any reading. Returns you to summary when done — no data loss.

±
± Neg Button

Appears on decimal readings. Toggles a minus sign prefix. Use this instead of typing "−" — the phone keyboard won't let you type a negative on a number pad.

Sending the Report
Text report — send it to your customer From the finished-job screen, tap Text report. The app builds a clean inspection certificate and opens your share sheet — send it straight to the customer by text or email. It arrives as an image, so it works on any phone, and it's drawn on your device so it works with no signal in the field.
Full report — the detailed PDF for records Tap Full report for the complete diagnostic version (every reading per cell, QR code, PASS / FAIL / REVIEW banner, bridge-deviation callouts, and ROC comparison). You'll be asked whether to include a signature block. Use your phone's share sheet to save it as a PDF or email it for your records.
Deleting a Job
Remove a job anytime On the Home or All History screen, tap the delete icon on any job card and confirm. You can delete a job whether it's finished or still in progress — handy for clearing out a mistake or a practice run.
Open TRAC Truck

Truck & vehicle scale calibration (Handbook 44). Runs a section test and an increasing/decreasing load test, guides you through the adjustment, and generates a PASS/FAIL PDF certificate with photos.

Result Status Reference
PASS

Sections: spread ≤ tolerance (60 lb default).
Load: every point within tolerance (20 lb default).

FAIL

Sections: spread > tolerance.
Load: any up/down reading misses tolerance.

PARTIAL

Not every section or load point has been entered yet — finish entering readings to get a final PASS/FAIL.

8-Step Calibration Walkthrough
1
Visit

Pick the scale (or add a new one). Choose Testing With: Truck (runs the section test) or Cart (runs the load test). Enter the certified cart weight, weight per drop, and # of drops.

The error math needs the certified cart weight — enter it here before moving on.
2
As-Found Sections

Drive the truck forward across each section (and reverse too, unless this branch is cart-only). Enter each reading — the app checks the spread across all readings against your tolerance.

Out of tolerance? The app names the high and low sections and which way to adjust.
3
As-Found Load

With the test cart, enter the increasing run (empty cart → add a rack → add another…) then the decreasing run back down. Empty cart is the top row, drops build down the screen.

Decreasing starts one rack below the top — you pull a rack before your first down-reading, so the top row's Down cell stays blank. That's expected.
4
Adjust

If either As-Found test came back out of tolerance, make your adjustment. If both already passed, tap "Yes — left as found" to skip straight to Photos and Review.

5
As-Left Sections

Re-run the section test after adjusting. Tap Copy As-Found numbers if a section you didn't touch should carry over.

6
As-Left Load

Re-run the load test the same way — empty cart on top, drops going down, decreasing starting one rack below the max.

7
Photos

Capture the nameplate, stickers, deck condition, and the cart/blocks used. These embed directly in the PDF certificate.

8
Review

Check the final PASS/FAIL summary for both tests, add sign-off if needed, then generate the PDF certificate.

PDF Certificate
Generating the certificate From the Review step, generate the PDF. It includes a QR code, PASS/FAIL banner, both tests (As-Found and As-Left), and your field photos — the same standard as TRAC Belt's Customer Report.
IndySoft entry is still manual There's currently no phone → IndySoft sync, so office cert numbers still get typed in on the Michelli Surface tablets. A native IndySoft mobile app was announced in 2026 — worth checking with the IndySoft rep on whether Michelli's license covers it.
Important — Your data lives on your device All three apps store all data in your phone's local browser storage. If you clear your browser data or switch phones, you'll lose your history unless you've made a backup.
Backup & Restore
Export Backup

Find Export backup at the bottom of the home screen in each app (TRAC Belt: Backup / restore data). Downloads a .json file with all your data. Save it to Files, iCloud, or email it to yourself. Do this regularly.

Import / Restore

Tap Import Backup and select your .json file. The app merges the data — importing the same file twice is safe, no duplicates.

Getting the Apps on Your Phone
Add to Home Screen (iPhone) 1. Open the app link in Safari (not Chrome).
2. Tap the Share icon (box with arrow).
3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
4. The app icon appears on your home screen — works offline once added.
